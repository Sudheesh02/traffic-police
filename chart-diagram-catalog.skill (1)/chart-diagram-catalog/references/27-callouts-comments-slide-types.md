# 27. Callouts, Comments & Slide Types

Annotation bubbles and status labels (Draft, Confidential, Illustrative, etc.).

**7 items in this category — IDs 254–260.**

| ID | Name | Structure | Common Uses | Unique / Creative Use | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 254 | Call-out | Speech-bubble or rectangular box with connecting line | Pointing to specific chart elements, adding notes | Pointing directly at the one outlier point on a scatter plot that skews the whole trend | Directs attention; adds explanation without clutter |
| 255 | Draft | Watermark or corner label | Early version slides | Watermarking an early thesis draft shared with an advisor before it's ready for full review | Sets expectation of incompleteness |
| 256 | Work in Progress | Same | Slides under development | Labeling an in-progress experiment's results slide so it isn't mistaken for a final number | Prevents premature review |
| 257 | Internal | Label | Internal-only content | Marking a lab's internal meeting notes so they aren't shared outside the group | Restricts distribution |
| 258 | Confidential | Label | Sensitive information | Marking a startup's cap table or financials before sharing with a potential investor | Legal/security requirement |
| 259 | Preliminary | Label | Initial analysis, subject to change | Labeling early, unreplicated experiment results shared at a weekly lab meeting | Manages stakeholder expectations |
| 260 | Illustrative | Label | Mock-ups, examples, not real data | Marking placeholder numbers in a pitch deck's financial-projections slide before real data exists | Avoids misinterpretation |
